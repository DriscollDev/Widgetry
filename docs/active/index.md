## What is Widgetry?

Most people check the same handful of things every day: the weather, a stock price, whether a website is online, the status of a service they depend on. Each of those lives in a different app or tab. Widgetry puts them in one place, arranged the way you want.

## Who is it for?

- **Developers** who want to keep an eye on their projects and APIs without standing up Grafana
- **Hobbyists** who want a personal "dashboard for my day"
- **The curious** who like pulling values from public data sources on the internet

## What can you do?

- Create up to ten **boards** for different purposes
- Add **widgets** from a catalog of seven types, or point a custom widget at almost any public API
- **Drag and resize** widgets on a grid layout
- View **history charts** for widgets that track data over time
- **Refresh** on a schedule you choose, or on demand

This guide walks you through signing up and placing your first widget. The whole process takes about five minutes.

## Create an account

1. Open Widgetry in your browser.
2. Choose **Sign up** and enter your email and password, or use **Sign in with Google**.
3. If you signed up with email, check your inbox for a verification link. You can use the app right away - verification is only required if you later need to reset your password.

## Your first board

After signing in, you land on your dashboard. If you're new, you'll see an empty **board** - a single page where widgets live.

1. Give your board a name, such as "Morning Check-in" or "Project Health."
2. You'll see an empty grid ready for widgets.

You can create up to ten boards. Most people end up with two or three for different purposes.

## Add your first widget

1. Click **Add widget** to open the catalog.
2. For a quick start, try the **Weather** widget:
   - Enter your city or location
   - Place it on the grid
3. Or try **Uptime** to monitor a website:
   - Enter the URL you want to check (for example, your project's homepage)
   - The widget will check it on a schedule and show whether it's responding

Each widget type asks a few simple questions when you add it. Weather wants a location; stocks want a symbol; uptime wants a URL.

## Arrange your board

- **Drag** a widget to move it on the grid
- **Drag the edges** to resize it - from a single cell up to a six-by-six block
- If you try to drop a widget on top of another, it will snap back with a brief flash. Find an empty spot instead.

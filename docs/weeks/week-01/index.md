The first week of planning established the project's premise: a customizable,
widget-based dashboard for monitoring APIs and surfacing live data. This week's
deliverable is the project proposal itself, written to give the capstone advisor a
clear sense of the problem we are solving, who would use it, and the technology
direction we expect to take.

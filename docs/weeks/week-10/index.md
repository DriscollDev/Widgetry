The capstone defense deck. Where the mid-term presentation in week 5 was a
checkpoint, this is the deliverable that ties the ten weeks of planning together
for review. The deck references this wiki as the artifact holding the
supporting work - week 9's deliverable - rather than trying to reproduce it
inline.

Suggested coverage: a brief project framing, a tour through the weekly
deliverables, the handful of design decisions most likely to draw challenge in
defense, how the risks identified during planning were addressed, and what the
ten-week development phase that follows planning is structured to deliver.

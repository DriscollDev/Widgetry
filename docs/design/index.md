A web application that lets you build a personal dashboard from data sources you care about - without writing code or running your own server.

## What is this?

Most people end up checking the same handful of things every day: the weather, a stock price, whether a website they care about is online, the status of a project they're following. Each of those lives in a different app or website. We're building a single page where you can put all of them in one place, arranged the way you want.

## Who is it for?

We designed it for three kinds of people: developers who want to keep an eye on their own projects, hobbyists who want a personal "dashboard for my day," and the curious who like aggregating information from public data sources. [More about our users →](/design/who-its-for/)

## What does it do?

You sign up, create a *board*, and place *widgets* on it. Each widget shows one thing - a temperature reading, a stock price, whether a website is responding, the value pulled from any public data source you point it at. You can resize widgets, rearrange them, and have multiple boards for different purposes. [Take a tour →](/design/what-it-does/)

## Who built it?

Our team of three students is working under the team name **Pokeballers**, as a 20-week capstone project. Ten weeks of planning produced the specifications and design documents this wiki is based on. The remaining ten weeks are for building. [Meet the team →](/design/team/)

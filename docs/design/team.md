## How we work

We meet as a team twice per week - once at the start of the sprint to plan, and once mid-sprint to check in and unblock each other. Code reviews happen continuously: every change goes through review by at least one other team member before it's merged. We try to keep our individual work in branches that don't live longer than a couple of days, so we don't end up with painful merges later.

For more on our process, see [Our Process](/design/planning-our-process/).
